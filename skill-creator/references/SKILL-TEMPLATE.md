## Coverage Tracking Protocol

**After every `generate` or `gap-fill` run:**
1. Mark skills as COVERED in `_coverage-map.md`
2. Update frontmatter: `covered`, `gap`, `coverage_pct`
3. Update the summary line: `**Total Skills**: N | **Covered**: X | **Gap**: Y | **Coverage**: Z%`

Target: **90%+ coverage** before the exam.
